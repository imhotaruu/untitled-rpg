# When the Stars Faltered

Player-facing script from the supplied V2 draft, with the user-approved Order/Chaos correction. Bracketed lines are presentation directions; tutorial mechanics are adapted in code.

Feature 35 presentation: the manuscript below remains the writing reference. In-game paragraphs are curated in `src/data/prologueScript.js`: adjacent narration and selected same-speaker lines are grouped, reducing 350 story/tutorial advances to 256 without removing prose. Dramatic reveals and speaker changes retain their pauses. Narration has no visible speaker label; named dialogue retains its nameplate. Starwake is the weapon; its physical attack command is now Starward Slash.

## SCENE 1: THE BEGINNING OF CREATION

**[Opening Cinematic — Black Screen]**

*For a moment, there is nothing.*

*No sound. No movement. No light.*

**Narrator:**

Before time was measured, there was only stillness.

From that stillness, two primordial truths emerged.

**Order and Chaos.**

Through Order, creation found its form. Stars illuminated the heavens, oceans embraced the land, and life began its endless journey.

Yet where creation flourished, Chaos lingered beyond its boundaries.

Within creation, life held countless possibilities. Mortals could choose, change, and shape futures even the gods could not foresee. But beyond its boundaries, Chaos stirred—a force that threatened to unravel it all.

**[Scene: The darkness gradually gives way to a vast celestial landscape. Stars are born, oceans form, forests spread across the land, and countless living beings emerge.]**

**Narrator:**

Ages passed, and civilizations rose beneath the heavens.

Mortals lived their fleeting lives, experiencing joy and sorrow, love and loss, triumph and despair.

But when calamities threatened the world, there were those who refused to turn away.

Ordinary people who placed the lives of others above their own.

Through extraordinary courage, sacrifice, and selflessness, these individuals were entrusted with powers beyond mortal comprehension.

They ascended, becoming the guardians of creation.

**The Divine Authorities.**

Once mortal themselves, they accepted the responsibility of overseeing the domains entrusted to them.

Some watched over life, while others guided those who had reached its end.

Some commanded the radiant sun, while others tended to the countless stars scattered across the night sky.

Though immortal, they carried with them the memories, emotions, and convictions of their mortal lives.

For they had not become gods to rule over the world.

They had become gods to protect it.

**[Scene: Silhouettes of the Divine Authorities appear against the heavens. A radiant sun rises, forests bloom, and constellations illuminate the sky.]**

**Narrator:**

For countless generations, the Divine Authorities maintained the fragile balance of creation.

But that peace would not last forever.

From beyond the boundaries of existence, primordial Chaos began to stir.

And with its awakening came a force that sought to unravel the very world the gods had sworn to protect.

**[The celestial landscape begins to fracture. Stars disappear into an expanding darkness.]**

**[Screen fades to black.]**

## SCENE 2: THE WAR AGAINST CHAOS

**[Scene: The Celestial Battlefield]**

*The heavens tremble.*

*Across the shattered firmament, countless divine figures battle against an overwhelming darkness.*

*Blinding bursts of magic illuminate the battlefield as the gods struggle to repel the advancing manifestations of Chaos.*

*The enemy possesses no singular, recognizable form. Its enormous presence twists and shifts beyond comprehension, while countless fragments break away from its mass, assuming monstrous shapes.*

*To gaze upon it is to feel as though something is staring directly into one's soul.*

*Amid the destruction, a lone goddess stands before an enormous celestial barrier.*

*Her long hair drifts through the weightless battlefield, illuminated by the scattered glow of dying stars.*

*Countless threads of starlight extend from her hands, weaving themselves into a barrier that restrains the encroaching darkness.*

*She is Astraea, the Goddess of Stars.*

**Astraea:** “Come on… just a little longer…!”

*The barrier trembles violently.*

**Astraea:** “Ah—!”

*She struggles to maintain her footing, her hands shaking from the tremendous strain.*

**Astraea:** “If anyone can hear me, I need assistance!”

*Her voice echoes across the battlefield.*

**Astraea:** “I can't hold this side much longer! Please, someone answer!”

*For a moment, there is no response.*

*Only the distant sounds of battle answer her plea.*

*Across the heavens, divine warriors continue to fall. Some disappear beneath the advancing darkness, while others sacrifice what remains of their strength to protect their companions.*

*Astraea watches helplessly as another distant light fades.*

**Astraea:** “No… not another one…”

*She closes her eyes briefly before tightening her grasp on the threads of starlight.*

**Astraea:** “Not yet. I won't let it end like this.”

*Suddenly, a familiar voice breaks through the chaos.*

**Morveth:** “ASTRAEA!”

*Astraea turns toward the source.*

*Beyond the broken light, three figures emerge from the battlefield.*

**Morveth:** “We're here! And I've brought some company!”

*Morveth, the God of Death, approaches with visible exhaustion. Despite his labored breathing, he manages a reassuring smile.*

**Astraea:** “Morveth?!”

*Her eyes widen as she recognizes the two figures accompanying him.*

**Astraea:** “Aurelion! Elly! You're all here!”

**Morveth:** “You called for help. Did you really think we'd leave you to handle this alone?”

**Aurelion:** “Though I wish we'd arrived under better circumstances.”

*Aurelion, the God of the Sun, looks toward the distant battlefield.*

*His usual confidence has faded. The light surrounding him flickers as he watches the remnants of fallen divine warriors disappear into the darkness.*

**Astraea:** “How are the others holding up?”

*Aurelion hesitates.*

**Aurelion:** “They're still fighting. The remaining gods are keeping the other manifestations occupied.”

*He lowers his gaze.*

**Aurelion:** “But… we've lost many.”

*Silence falls between them.*

**Astraea:** “…I see.”

*Her expression tightens as she looks toward the fading lights in the distance.*

**Astraea:** “They gave everything to protect this world…”

**Morveth:** “And we must make sure their sacrifices weren't in vain.”

*Astraea looks back toward her companions.*

*She takes a breath, then nods.*

**Astraea:** “You're right.”

**Elaris:** “Now, now. As much as I'd love for us to have a heartfelt reunion, our dearest Astraea looks like she's about to collapse!”

*Elaris, the Goddess of Life, hurries to Astraea's side.*

**Elaris:** “Honestly, dear. You really must stop trying to carry the heavens on your own.”

**Astraea:** “E-Elly! I wasn't trying to!”

**Elaris:** “Oh? Then why do you look like you've been holding this barrier by yourself for ages?”

*Astraea gives an awkward laugh.*

**Astraea:** “Well… when you put it that way…”

*Elaris sighs fondly before placing a hand against Astraea's shoulder.*

*A gentle emerald radiance envelops the exhausted goddess.*

**[Elaris uses divine healing magic.]**

*The trembling in Astraea's hands gradually subsides as strength returns to her body.*

**Astraea:** “Ahh… that's much better!”

*She smiles brightly.*

**Astraea:** “Thanks, Elly! I really needed that!”

**Elaris:** “You're very welcome, darling.”

*She turns toward the other two gods.*

**Elaris:** “Now then, would you two gentlemen be so kind as to help us reinforce this seal?”

**Morveth:** “Of course.”

**Aurelion:** “Right. Let's finish what we came here to do.”

## SCENE 3: THE FRACTURED SEAL

**[Scene: The Four Divine Authorities Gather]**

*Morveth and Aurelion take their positions beside Astraea and Elaris.*

*Four distinct forms of divine energy begin to converge.*

*Brilliant starlight intertwines with the golden radiance of the sun. Gentle currents of life energy flow through the barrier, while Morveth's solemn power anchors its weakening structure.*

*For the first time since the battle began, the celestial barrier stabilizes.*

**Astraea:** “It's working!”

**Aurelion:** “Of course it is! With all four of us here, what could possibly—”

*A deafening crack interrupts him.*

**[A violent shockwave erupts from the barrier.]**

**Aurelion:** “—go wrong?!”

**Morveth:** “Really, Aurelion?!”

**Aurelion:** “Oh, come on! I didn't do that!”

*The barrier shudders.*

*An enormous fracture spreads across its surface, and a fragment of Chaos forces its way through the opening.*

*The fragment twists and writhes as it falls onto the shattered battlefield.*

*Slowly, its shapeless body begins to resemble a fallen soldier.*

*Its limbs move unnaturally, and its hollow eyes shine with an unsettling darkness.*

**Morveth:** “Damn it! One of them got through!”

**Aurelion:** “If it reaches the other side, the entire seal could collapse!”

**Elaris:** “We can't abandon our positions! The barrier's still unstable!”

*Astraea watches the creature rise.*

*For a moment, her expression softens as she observes its resemblance to the fallen warrior.*

**Astraea:** “…Even the fallen aren't allowed to rest in peace.”

*She slowly withdraws her hands from the barrier.*

**Elaris:** “Astraea?”

**Astraea:** “You three keep sealing Chaos. I'll take care of this one.”

**Morveth:** “Are you certain? You've barely recovered!”

*Astraea turns toward him with a reassuring smile.*

**Astraea:** “I'll be alright. Besides, if we let that thing wander around, all our hard work will have been for nothing.”

**Aurelion:** “Just don't push yourself too hard!”

**Elaris:** “And please come back in one piece, dear!”

**Astraea:** “Of course!”

*She takes a few steps toward the approaching fragment before looking over her shoulder.*

**Astraea:** “I'm counting on you three!”

**Morveth:** “You can count on us.”

**Aurelion:** “Leave the barrier to us!”

**Elaris:** “Go get them, darling!”

*Astraea smiles.*

*Then, turning toward the creature, she extends her right hand.*

*Countless motes of starlight gather around her fingertips.*

*The scattered lights intertwine, slowly forming the outline of a magnificent sword.*

*As she closes her fingers around its hilt, the weapon awakens.*

*Its blade shimmers like the night sky, with delicate constellations flowing along its edge.*

**Astraea:** “Come forth…”

**Astraea:** “**Starwake.**”

**[Starwake — The Celestial Blade — materializes in Astraea's hand.]**

*The goddess raises her weapon toward the advancing darkness.*

**Astraea:** “I made a promise to protect this world.”

*Her expression becomes resolute.*

**Astraea:** “And I intend to keep it.”

## SCENE 5: THE LAST SEAL

**[Cinematic Resumes — Player Control Ends]**

*The celestial barrier is nearing its breaking point.*

*Although the three remaining gods have successfully stabilized its foundation, the sheer force of Chaos continues to overwhelm their efforts.*

**Morveth:** “We can't hold it like this forever!”

**Aurelion:** “The energy's slipping through! We need one final push!”

**Elaris:** “Astraea!”

*Astraea rushes back toward her companions.*

**Astraea:** “I'm here!”

*She raises Starwake.*

*The celestial blade dissolves into countless stars that gather around her hands.*

*Astraea closes her eyes.*

*For a brief moment, distant memories return to her.*

*An ordinary home beneath the evening sky.*

*The laughter of people she once knew.*

*The warmth of a life long since left behind.*

*She remembers the world as she knew it before her ascension.*

*There are days when she misses that life.*

*But as she opens her eyes and looks upon the world below, she remembers why she chose this path.*

*Countless people still live beneath those same stars.*

*People who laugh, who dream, who love, and who hope to see another tomorrow.*

*And as long as she has the power to protect them, she will never turn away.*

**Astraea:** “Everyone…”

*The stars around her begin to shine brighter.*

**Astraea:** “One last time. Together!”

**Morveth:** “With you until the end.”

**Aurelion:** “Let's show this thing what we're made of!”

**Elaris:** “Together, dears!”

*The four gods release their remaining divine energy.*

*Astraea's starlight spreads across the heavens, forming an immense celestial constellation.*

*Aurelion's radiance illuminates the fractured barrier.*

*Elaris restores its weakened structure, while Morveth anchors the seal against the relentless pressure of Chaos.*

*The surrounding heavens tremble as the barrier begins to close.*

*From beyond the seal, the incomprehensible presence of Chaos presses against their combined power.*

*For an instant, the darkness seems to look directly at Astraea.*

*And then—*

**[A tremendous burst of celestial light fills the screen.]**

*The fractures disappear.*

*The darkness retreats behind the barrier.*

*The heavens become still.*

**[The primordial Chaos has been sealed.]**

*For a long moment, none of the gods speak.*

*Their exhausted figures remain suspended above the world, surrounded by countless stars.*

**Aurelion:** “…Did we do it?”

**Morveth:** “The seal is holding.”

**Elaris:** “Oh, thank goodness…”

*Astraea slowly lowers her hands.*

**Astraea:** “We did it…”

*She looks toward her companions.*

*A tired but genuine smile appears on her face.*

**Astraea:** “We really did it!”

*Despite their exhaustion, the gods share a moment of relief.*

*Yet their victory is bittersweet.*

*Beyond the battlefield, countless lights that once belonged to their companions are gone.*

*And though Chaos has been restrained, its presence has not vanished entirely.*

*The seal will need to be watched over.*

*Perhaps for centuries.*

*Perhaps for eternity.*

**Morveth:** “Then our duty isn't over.”

*Astraea nods.*

**Astraea:** “No.”

*Her gaze drifts toward the distant world below.*

**Astraea:** “But at least… they can see another tomorrow.”

### The Lingering Thread

*As the gods prepare to depart, Astraea notices something unusual.*

*A faint thread of starlight remains stretched between her fingertips and the newly formed celestial seal.*

*She gently moves her hand.*

*The thread follows.*

**Astraea:** “Hm…?”

**Morveth:** “Something wrong?”

*Astraea studies the curious strand of light.*

**Astraea:** “Not exactly.”

*She looks toward the enormous barrier.*

**Astraea:** “It seems a little of my starlight remained within the seal.”

**Elaris:** “Oh, dear. Is that dangerous?”

**Astraea:** “I don't think so. The seal is stable, at least for now.”

*She gives the thread a gentle tug.*

*It pulses faintly, almost as though responding to her.*

**Aurelion:** “Well, that's certainly unusual.”

**Morveth:** “We should examine it once we've recovered. We don't yet know what maintaining this seal will demand of us.”

**Astraea:** “You're right. We'll keep an eye on it.”

*She smiles toward her companions.*

**Astraea:** “For now, though, I think we've all earned a little rest.”

**Elaris:** “Finally! Something sensible!”

*Aurelion laughs quietly.*

*As the others turn to leave, Astraea glances back toward the seal one last time.*

*The thread of starlight shimmers.*

*Then it becomes nearly invisible.*

*Unbeknownst to the gods, a connection has been formed that will endure long after the battlefield falls silent.*

## SCENE 6: A PROMISE BENEATH THE STARS

**[Scene Transition — The Passage of Ages]**

**Narrator:**

And so, the war against primordial Chaos came to an end.

The surviving Divine Authorities returned to their domains, mourning those they had lost and rebuilding what the conflict had destroyed.

Generations passed.

Kingdoms rose and fell. Civilizations flourished, and the memory of the great celestial war slowly became legend.

Yet high above the mortal world, the Divine Authorities continued their watch.

And among them, Astraea remained faithful to her promise.

**[Scene: The Quiet Heavens — Centuries Later]**

*Far below, the mortal world continues to turn.*

*Dawn approaches. The first rays of sunlight touch the mountains, rivers, villages, and forests that the gods once fought so desperately to protect.*

*Astraea stands among the stars, watching over the world.*

*Her celestial blade, Starwake, rests peacefully beside her, its form shimmering faintly in the darkness.*

*She gazes down at the countless lives unfolding beneath the heavens.*

*Then, something catches her attention.*

*A faint glimmer.*

*Not a star, but a tiny spark of life somewhere within the mortal world.*

*It is fragile, barely distinguishable among the countless souls below.*

*Yet something about its gentle radiance draws her attention.*

**Astraea:** “Hm…?”

*She tilts her head slightly.*

**Astraea:** “What a curious little light…”

*The spark flickers softly.*

*For reasons she cannot explain, Astraea feels compelled to watch it a little longer.*

*She smiles.*

**Astraea:** “Hello there, little one.”

*She reaches toward the distant light, though it lies far beyond her grasp.*

**Astraea:** “I wonder what kind of life awaits you.”

*The stars above her shimmer.*

*Her expression softens.*

**Astraea:** “You know… there was a time when I lived beneath these very same stars.”

*She lets out a quiet laugh.*

**Astraea:** “I worried about such ordinary things back then. What to eat, where to go, who I might see the next morning…”

*For a moment, she closes her eyes.*

**Astraea:** “I do miss those days.”

*She opens them again, smiling warmly.*

**Astraea:** “But I wouldn't trade this duty for anything.”

*Her gaze returns to the small light below.*

**Astraea:** “Because somewhere out there, there are still countless lives waiting to begin.”

*A gentle silence follows.*

**Astraea:** “Little one… I do not know what tomorrow shall ask of you.”

*The faint light flickers once more.*

**Astraea:** “Perhaps you will live a peaceful life, surrounded by the people you cherish.”

“Perhaps you will wander far beyond the home you have yet to know.”

“Or perhaps, someday, you will face trials greater than anything you could ever imagine.”

*She looks toward the distant heavens.*

*For just an instant, the ancient celestial seal glimmers faintly among the stars.*

*The thread of starlight connecting her to it still remains.*

*Astraea watches it, thoughtfully.*

*Then her gaze returns to the small light below.*

**Astraea:** “And should the day come when even the stars struggle to illuminate your path…”

*She places a hand over her heart.*

**Astraea:** “Remember that you need not face the darkness alone.”

“The bonds you forge, the kindness you share, and the people you choose to protect…”

*Her smile brightens.*

**Astraea:** “Those things may become a light greater than any star I could ever place upon the heavens.”

*The goddess extends her hand.*

*A single mote of starlight descends toward the mortal world.*

**Astraea:** “Whatever future you choose, little one…”

**Astraea:** “May you find happiness beneath these stars.”

*Her voice becomes a gentle whisper.*

**Astraea:** “And may they always guide you home.”

**[The mote of starlight descends through the clouds.]**

**[Screen slowly fades to white.]**

*For a brief moment, everything becomes silent.*

*Then—*

**[A newborn baby's cry echoes.]**

*The white light gradually fades, revealing the beginning of an ordinary life.*

**Narrator:**

And so, beneath the watchful gaze of the heavens…

A new story begins.
